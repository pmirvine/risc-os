HostFS
======

HostFS lets you use folders on the computer running your web browser as
if they were discs in RISC OS: HostFS::<name>.$ Each mounted folder has
an icon on the left of the icon bar, like the hard disc's.

!HostFS is how you mount them. Run it when you need it: its icon
appears on the right of the icon bar.

  SELECT on the icon    mount a folder (your browser asks which)
  ADJUST on the icon    the Mounts window
  MENU on the icon      Info, Mounts..., Mount folder..., Mount
                        read-only..., Server folders, Quit

The Mounts window lists every folder mounted or remembered. Choose one,
then Open, Dismount, Mount or Forget it; "Mount" (at start-up) says
whether it's mounted again whenever the desktop starts. A folder you
dismount stays in the list, not mounted at start-up, until you Forget
it.

Quitting !HostFS leaves the folders mounted. You can also mount with
the commands *HostMount, *HostDismount and *HostMounts, or by dropping
a folder from your computer onto the desktop.

The guide Docs.HostFS on the hard disc explains it all.
